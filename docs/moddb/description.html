The whole smoking culture for Vintage Story 1.22. Grow tobacco, cure it, shred it and roll it by hand or by the dozen. Smoke cigarettes, cigars, pipes or a vaporizer that runs on temporal gears. Collect nineteen parody brands in hand-dyed packs (one of them rots). Climb the 71-achievement Smoker's Ledger for character traits and server-wide shame. Then flick the butt onto the ground and watch the pile grow.

Every item description doubles as a health warning. Your lungs called. They want a divorce.

Grow it yourself

  • Three tobacco varieties (Virginia, Burley, Oriental), each with its own seeds, nutrient, climate range, leaves and shredded tobacco. The last growth stages stand 1.5 blocks tall.
  • Mint (for menthol crystals) and clove shrubs, for menthols and kreteks.
  • Fresh leaves dry in 96 hours on their own, or six times faster hung on a drying rack (4 leaves) or drying frame (12 leaves). A knife shreds a dried leaf into 2 shredded tobacco.

Batch furniture

  • Tobacco cutter: shreds up to 32 dried leaves in one crank.
  • Rolling table: 10 cigarettes per paper, where rolling by hand gives 3. Add menthol crystals or cloves for menthols and kreteks.
  • Right click with ingredients to load, hold right click with an empty hand to work, sneak + right click to unload.

Things to smoke, and things to light them with

  • Hand-rolled cigarettes, menthols, kreteks that crackle and spit embers, cigars, stub cigars, and snipes rolled from six old butts.
  • Wooden and bone pipes, packed with shredded tobacco from your offhand.
  • The Jool temporal vaporizer. It runs on real e-liquid brewed in a barrel (plain, menthol or clove), loaded as glass cartridges or straight from a bucket, jug or barrel. Charge it with a temporal gear (300 puffs) or a rusty gear (40 puffs).
  • Light up with a torch, lantern, matches, a Zappo lighter, or another lit cigarette (chain smoking). Matches and lighters in the main hand light firepits and anything else a firestarter can, instantly.
  • Lit cigarettes glow faintly in the dark.

19 parody brands

  • Marlbury, Camul, Lucky Stroke, Pall Moll, Winstun, Newpart, Koal, Djarun Black, Dunhull International, Bensun & Hodges Gold, Parlement, Gaulwaz Brunes, Kant, Vague Slims, Sobrani Cocktail, Belomorkanaal, Mild Eleven, Chesterfeld and Rotmans.
  • Each has its own blend, strength, slogan and one of twelve Seraph General's Warnings. "Kant: act only according to that maxim whereby you can will that everyone coughs."
  • Packs are coloured with the game's own dyes. Filtered brands take 10 cigarette butts as the filter. Yes, really.
  • Right click a pack to shake one out. Empty packs can be refilled with any cigarettes, and blank parchment packs can be dyed into any brand.
  • Rotmans is made from rot and comes in a pack that rots in your inventory. Matured to perfection, then a bit further.

Butts, flicked and otherwise

  • Every smoke leaves a butt. Flick it: hold right click to aim, release to send it tumbling through the air.
  • It lands on the ground in a pile that grows to 64 butts per block. Land one in an ashtray and you unlock a secret achievement.
  • The ashtray holds 12 butts, fills up visibly, and spills everywhere when broken.
  • Sneak + right click puts cigarettes, packs, lighters, pipes, leaves and herbs down on the ground as small decorations.

Effects

  • Each puff gives a 10-minute buff (less hunger, faster mining) scaled by the brand's strength.
  • Nicotine builds up. Go too long without and you get a mild withdrawal: a little hungrier, a little slower, and some cravings in chat. Overdo it and you start coughing.

The Smoker's Ledger

  • 71 achievements in 7 categories, including 11 hidden ones, prerequisite chains, streaks, collections and situational ones (smoking in a temporal storm, while on fire, at 2 HP, next to a trader...).
  • 8 ranks, from Curious Seraph to The Marlbury Man. Every promotion is announced to the whole server. Their family has been notified.
  • Some ranks send rewards from the Tobacco Board (rolling papers, a Golden Zappo, cigars), and 13 achievements grant permanent character traits like Smoker's Lungs, Deadeye and Death Wish.
  • /smokestats shows your progress, /smokestats list every achievement, /smokestats ranks the ladder.

Handbook

Eleven guide pages in the in-game handbook (Guides tab) cover tobacco, curing, rolling, smoking, pipes, butts, brands, dyes, vaping and the Ledger.
